01   The Unit

Every Hype Tradoor is a self-contained vector artwork, matching a 1000×1000 raster, and one metadata file listing its 8 traits and rarity tier. Metadata is written directly into the collection, nothing about it changes after mint.

04   Contract & Royalty

CHAIN: HyperEVM. STANDARD: ERC-721. SYMBOL: HYPETRADOORS. SUPPLY: Fixed at 3,333, cannot move.

ROYALTY: 6% on every secondary sale, enforced on-chain, live the moment trading opens. This funds the project treasury. Nothing more is claimed about its use beyond that today.

05   $TRADOORS & Staking

$TRADOORS is the project's designated token. The staking mechanics tied to it are in documentation, not deployed. Full detail on the Staking page.

The direction: staked Tradoors are planned to earn a share of the 6% royalty and $TRADOORS related fees, split across everyone staked, weighted by rarity. The long-term ambition is for payouts to eventually reflect real market exposure. Neither is live or funded today.

06   Questions

Who made this?
Hype Tradoors is an independent project built and operated by the team behind the @HypeTradoors account on X.
Why 3,333?
A fixed, deliberately scoped supply, small enough that individual rarity means something, large enough to support a real secondary market.
Can I use my Tradoor commercially?
Standard NFT usage terms apply: you own the specific token and the rights that come with holding it. Full commercial licensing terms will be published before any commercial use is assumed.
Is any of this on chain?
The mint, the 3,333 units, and the 6% royalty are all live and on-chain on HyperEVM today. Staking and $TRADOORS fee-routing are not deployed yet, see the Staking page for the honest current state.
How is rarity calculated?
Full breakdown on the Rarity page: every unit's score sums how rare each of its 8 traits is individually, then units are ranked and cut into five tiers at the real distribution's natural breakpoints.
Where do I check my reveal?
The Reveal page. Enter your token ID and it reads directly from the real collection metadata, no marketplace cache delay.
