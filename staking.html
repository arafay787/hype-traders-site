STAKING ISN'T
LIVE YET.

We'd rather tell you that plainly than let a page like this quietly imply otherwise. No contract exists today. No Tradoor can be staked right now. Here's what's actually happening instead.

01

IN DOCUMENTATION. The staking mechanics are being written and designed now, not deployed. Active roadmap item, not a vague someday.

02

THE DIRECTION. A staked Tradoor is planned to earn a share of the 6% secondary royalty and $TRADOORS related fees, split across everyone staked, weighted by rarity.

03

THE AMBITION. Because a Tradoor is a trader watching real markets, the long-term goal is for payouts to eventually reflect real market exposure, not an arbitrary reward token.

04

WHEN IT SHIPS. This page gets replaced with the real thing, a working contract, a connect-wallet flow, live numbers. Not a mockup dressed up as a product.

Why no countdown timer or rate shown: a lot of projects publish a polished "staking soon" page with numbers before anything is built, and it reads as a promise. This page states no rate, no date, no reward figure, because none of that is decided or deployed yet. When it is, this page changes, and you'll see exactly what changed.

STAKING QUESTIONS

When does staking go live?
No date is set. Publishing a date before the contract exists would be exactly the kind of promise this page is trying to avoid. This page updates the moment there's a real answer.
What will I earn if I stake?
The plan is a share of the 6% secondary royalty plus $TRADOORS related fees, weighted by rarity, so a Mythic pulls more than a Common. No exact rate exists yet because the contract isn't built.
Do I need to unstake before selling?
Once the contract exists, the plan is a standard stake and unstake flow with no lock-up penalty. This will be confirmed exactly once the contract is live, not before.
Is $TRADOORS live yet?
The token exists as the project's designated token. The fee-routing and staking mechanics tied to it are still in documentation, not deployed.
